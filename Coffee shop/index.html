Our Story

More than coffee.
It's a community.

The Daily Grind was born from a simple idea: Princeton deserved a place to slow down. We opened our doors with a commitment to quality beans, house-made syrups, and a space where everyone feels welcome — whether you're here for a quick pick-me-up or a long afternoon with your laptop.

Every cup is made to order by baristas who genuinely love what they do. We source from local roasters when we can, bake fresh every morning, and keep the wifi fast and the seats comfortable.

Locally sourced beans, roasted fresh
Baked goods made from scratch every morning
A welcoming space for everyone in the community
