Our story

From a garage to 34 communities

Bright Horizons Foundation was founded in 2008 by a group of nurses and teachers who saw, firsthand, how lack of clean water was keeping children out of school and trapping families in cycles of preventable illness. What began as weekend water-kit distributions from a garage in Johnston County has grown into a regional force for good.

Today we operate two flagship programs, partner with 12 local health systems, and employ a passionate team of 18 full-time staff — supported by 240 volunteers who give their time every single week.

Core initiatives

What we do every day

Each program is designed with the community's input, rigorously evaluated, and built to create sustainable outcomes — not just short-term relief.

Initiative 1

Clean Water Access Program

We install low-cost water filtration systems in homes and community centers, distribute certified water-quality kits, and train local leaders in maintenance and hygiene education. Since 2010, we have eliminated waterborne illness from 14 partner communities. Each installation comes with a 2-year maintenance guarantee and ongoing community health check-ins.

Initiative 2

Youth Education & Nutrition Initiative

Our after-school centers provide tutoring, hot nutritious meals, and social-emotional support for children ages 5–18. We partner with local schools and healthcare providers to ensure no child falls through the cracks due to hunger or learning gaps. Students in our program show a 32% improvement in reading proficiency within the first academic year.
