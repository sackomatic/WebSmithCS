What We Do

Our Services

No job too big, no detail too small. We handle residential and commercial projects across Johnston County.

Roofing

Full roof replacements, repairs, gutters, and inspections. We use top-grade materials and back every roof with a workmanship warranty.

Room Additions

Need more space? We design and build seamless additions that match your existing home — from sunrooms to full in-law suites.

Decks & Patios

Custom deck and patio builds — wood, composite, and concrete. Built to code, built to last, and built to make your backyard shine.

Kitchen & Bath Remodels

Full gut-to-finish kitchen and bathroom remodels. New layouts, tile, cabinets, countertops, fixtures — we handle every trade.

Siding & Exterior

Vinyl, HardiePlank, wood — we replace and repair all exterior siding types. We also handle exterior painting, shutters, and trim.

Flooring

Hardwood, LVP, tile, and carpet installation and refinishing. We prep the subfloor right so your floors last for decades.
