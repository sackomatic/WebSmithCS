Our Story

Built by the
Community,
for the Community

IronForge started as a single-room gym on Oak Street in 2011. We opened our doors because we were tired of driving 30 minutes to a big-box gym that didn't know our names.

Today, we're still the same gym at heart — local, personal, and proud of it. Our coaches know your name, your goals, and what makes you show up on the hard days. We're not trying to be the biggest gym in town. We're trying to be the best one for you.
