Since 1987

A Market Built on Community

Grove Market started as a simple farmstand on Elm Street, founded by the Calloway family with a single goal: to bring the freshest local food to our neighbors. Three generations later, that commitment hasn't wavered for a moment.

We work directly with over 30 local farms within a 50-mile radius, ensuring that what you put on your family's table is grown with care — not shipped across the country in a refrigerator truck.

Every season brings new faces and old friends through our doors. We know your name, we know your favorites, and we'll always go the extra mile to find what you're looking for.
