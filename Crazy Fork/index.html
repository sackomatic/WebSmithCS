Our Story

Good food,
good people.

The Cozy Fork started as a dream — a place where neighbors could gather over plates of honest, delicious food. We source our ingredients from local farms within 50 miles, cook everything from scratch daily, and treat every guest like family.


Whether it's a weeknight dinner or a special celebration, we're honored to be part of your story. Come hungry, leave happy — that's our promise.
